# Members Time — Give & Play Credit and Cancellation Rules
**Draft member-facing policy — 26 October 2026. Subject to legal review and publication approval.**

## 1. Credits and hosted rounds
Each eligible, completed hosted round earns the host **1.5 credits**, whether one or two visiting Members Time members attend. This applies equally to all eligible clubs, including ultra-exclusive and top-ranked clubs. Each visiting member uses **1 credit** for their place. For a pair, each visitor must have their own approved membership and their own credit.

A member may earn no more than **6 hosting credits per calendar month**. Credits already held in a wallet do **not** reset at the end of a month. Earning limits are not balance limits. Credits are a membership benefit, not money, cannot be exchanged for cash, and do not guarantee a tee time.

## 2. When credits are reserved
When a host accepts a booking, **1 credit per visiting member is reserved**. Reserved credits cannot be used for other bookings. No hosting credits are earned merely for accepting a booking. The host and visitors must comply with the host club's guest, handicap, dress and conduct rules.

## 3. Host cancellation
If a host cancels an accepted round before completion, each visitor's reserved credit is **released automatically and immediately** for reuse. No host credits are earned. This also applies when the host cancels a booking for two visitors. Members do not need to wait for a new calendar month.

If a released credit would otherwise have expired before **30 days after the host's cancellation**, its use-by date will be extended to that date. This protection applies only to credits actually affected by the cancelled booking; it is not a new bonus credit.

## 4. Completion and credit settlement
Once the host and the visiting member(s) confirm that the round took place, each visitor's reserved credit is settled as spent and the host earns **1.5 credits for that round in total**, subject to the monthly earning cap. For a pair, all participating members must confirm before automatic settlement. A round cannot be rewarded twice.

## 5. Cancellations by visitors and disputes
A visitor who needs to cancel should notify the host promptly. **Visitor-cancellation deadlines, no-show consequences, weather disruption and dispute handling are not yet finalised**; until published, these cases must be reviewed manually and must not result in an automatic credit forfeiture. If the host and visitor disagree whether a round took place, credits remain on hold pending review.

## 6. Repeated cancellations and misuse
Members who repeatedly cancel accepted rounds, misrepresent completion, or attempt to manipulate credits may have booking privileges restricted following a review. Members should have a reasonable opportunity to explain or appeal a restriction.

## 7. Technical and launch status
This policy describes the intended rules. Automatic reservation, release, expiry extension and settlement require the secure transaction workflow to be implemented and tested before the feature is enabled. Existing credit balances must not be changed merely by publishing this draft.

## 8. General terms
Members Time facilitates introductions and reciprocal hosting; actual play is subject to host consent, course availability, club rules and any applicable guest fees. Members must act respectfully and provide accurate eligibility information. The final member terms should also address privacy, membership fees, liability, complaints and applicable UK consumer law. This draft is **not** a complete set of website Terms and Conditions and should be reviewed by a UK solicitor before launch.
