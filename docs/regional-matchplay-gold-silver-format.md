# Members Time Match Play Championship — regional Gold Cup / Silver Plate concept

## Competition structure (proposed; not live)
- Eligible, independently verified Members Time golfers enter geographically allocated regional knockout brackets. Prefer local ties within roughly 30–40 miles when numbers permit; club eligibility is based on the Members Time approved club list, not a promise of 100 clubs in each county.
- Regional knockout ties are two legs of 18-hole singles match play, one at each player's home course, subject to both clubs permitting visitor access and applicable fees.
- Each leg is independently scored as 1 point for a win, 0.5 for a halved match, 0 for a loss. A 1–1 aggregate tie requires a published playoff rule agreed before the season; do not invent aggregate-hole margins or decide after play.
- Handicap and tee/course-rating rules must follow current applicable WHS and Rules of Golf guidance, with published maximum index, allowances, and score-reporting requirements.
- Regional champions advance to the national **MT Gold Cup**. Regional runners-up advance to the national **MT Silver Plate**. Both are separate national knockout competitions.
- Consider neutral-venue regional finals, and national finals as organised events. Venue access, guest fees, travel, weather, withdrawals and disputes require published rules.

## Suggested initial regional groupings
Dorset/New Forest; Hampshire/West Sussex; Surrey/South London; Kent/East Sussex; Berkshire/Oxfordshire/Buckinghamshire; Somerset/Devon/Cornwall; Midlands; Hertfordshire/Bedfordshire/Cambridgeshire/Essex; Northern England (subdivide by entries); Wales; Scotland; Ireland (subdivide by entries). All boundaries provisional, to be determined by eligible entrants and travel practicality.

## Bracket integrity and scaling
- A region's bracket may use seeded byes if the entrant count is not a power of two. Define transparent seeding/draw rules and publish before entries open.
- National Gold and Silver bracket sizes may differ; use explicit byes or a preliminary round rather than implying every region will have identical entries.
- A player cannot enter multiple regional brackets in the same season.
- Match completion requires both competitors to confirm the result or an admin adjudication, with audit trail.
- Design the site to show region selection, registration status, draw, leg results, deadlines, advancement and national brackets.
- No registration or fee collection until rules, eligibility, safeguarding, privacy, and payment arrangements are approved.

**Status:** specification only; no live matchplay functionality or database changes.
