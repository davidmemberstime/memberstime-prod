# Members Time Pairs Match Play Championship — regional Gold Cup / Silver Plate

**Status:** Proposed competition specification only. No live brackets, registrations, payment, or database changes.

## Separate from singles
Run two independent championship programmes:
1. **Singles:** Regional singles champions → MT Gold Cup; regional singles runners-up → MT Silver Plate.
2. **Pairs:** Regional pairs champions → **MT Pairs Gold Cup**; regional pairs runners-up → **MT Pairs Silver Plate**.

A member may enter both singles and pairs, subject to schedules and eligibility. Each pairs team comprises two individually verified, eligible Members Time members. **Pairs teammates do not need to be linked £29.50 billing partners**; they may be any two confirmed golfing friends. A team cannot change its two players after entries close except under a published replacement rule.

## Regional structure
Use the same provisional geographic regions as singles, allocating each pair to one region by a published rule (e.g. primary member's home club, with manual review for cross-region teams). Aim for practical 30–40 mile travel distances where possible. Bracket sizes, byes and regional divisions depend on entries.

## Home and away
Each knockout tie comprises two 18-hole pairs match-play legs, with each team hosting at its nominated home club where permitted. Both visiting players must be admitted under club rules; green fees are not automatically waived. Both legs need prepublished handicap, tee, format, result confirmation and tie-break rules. **Pairs playing format (four-ball better-ball versus foursomes) and handicap allowance are still to be decided**; do not publish a definite scoring rule until confirmed.

Regional pairs winners progress to MT Pairs Gold Cup, regional pairs runners-up to MT Pairs Silver Plate. The national tournaments are independent from singles Gold/Silver.

## Product requirements
- Member can form a pairs team with an accepted friend; second golfer must confirm.
- Check both memberships, home clubs and competition eligibility.
- Separate singles and pairs registration, draws, regional standings, result reporting and national brackets.
- Prevent one golfer being entered on multiple pairs teams in the same season.
- Show team names, clubs, dates, two leg results, advancement, deadline and disputes.
- Admin tools for draws, cancellations, substitutions, walkovers and verified results.
- Do not assume billing partnership equals pairs championship eligibility.
