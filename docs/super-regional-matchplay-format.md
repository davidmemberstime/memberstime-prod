# Members Time — super-regional qualifying stage (proposal)

**Status:** Development-only competition specification; no live registration, draws, database changes, or published competition rules.

## Three-stage competition ladder
1. **Local regional brackets:** roughly 30–40-mile catchments where practical. Singles and pairs competitions each use two-leg home-and-away match play. Each regional bracket produces a champion and runner-up.
2. **Super-regional qualifiers:** wider geographical groupings bringing together regional finalists from different local areas, creating opportunities to play eligible prestigious clubs members have not visited before.
3. **National finals:** super-regional qualifiers advance into the national **MT Gold Cup**, **MT Silver Plate**, **MT Pairs Gold Cup**, and **MT Pairs Silver Plate**.

## Recommended parallel tracks
- **Gold track:** local regional champions compete against other local champions in super-regional Gold brackets. Super-regional Gold winners qualify for the national Gold Cup.
- **Silver track:** local regional runners-up compete against other runners-up in super-regional Silver brackets. Super-regional Silver winners qualify for the national Silver Plate.
- Both singles and pairs follow these separate tracks. No member/team is automatically eliminated from all national prospects solely because they finished runner-up locally.
- Where needed for fair national bracket sizes, allocate additional qualification spots or byes using rules published before the season. Do not promise a fixed number of finalists before region sizes are known.

## Format decision
Prefer **knockout brackets** initially: simpler scheduling, fewer compulsory away trips and clear qualification. A four-player/four-team round robin is an optional later variant where participants can reasonably complete three fixtures. The final choice should be made before registrations and match deadlines are announced.

## Fairness and experience
- Draws should prioritize cross-region pairings during super-regionals, rather than rematches between players from the same local bracket.
- Preserve home-and-away opportunity where club access permits, or publish neutral-venue alternatives.
- Visiting rounds require club permission and may incur guest/green fees; Members Time membership does not guarantee access.
- Publish WHS handicap allowances, tees, match reporting, tie breaks, fixture deadlines, substitutions and dispute resolution before launch.
- Pairs use four-ball better-ball match play, subject to final published handicap rules; pairs partners need not be linked billing partners.
- Website must show local → super-regional → national bracket progress and qualification status for singles and pairs separately.
